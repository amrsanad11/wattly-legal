Effective date: October 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Wattly (“Wattly”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you use the Wattly mobile application and related services.

Questions? Contact amr.sanad.eg@gmail.com or 01104615280.

1. Information we collect

Account information. When you sign in with Google, we receive information made available by Google, such as your name, email address, profile photo, and unique account identifier.

App and usage information. We may process information you enter in the app, preferences, device and app version, language, approximate technical information, and activity required to operate and secure Wattly.

Notifications. If enabled, Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) provides a device registration token so Wattly can deliver notifications. You can disable notifications in device settings.

Advertising. Google AdMob may collect device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP address, and interaction information to provide and measure ads, subject to your settings and applicable law. See Google’s partner policy.

2. How we use information

  • Provide, maintain, and personalize Wattly features.
  • Authenticate users, prevent abuse, troubleshoot, and secure the service.
  • Send notifications you request or that are necessary for the service.
  • Understand performance and improve Wattly.
  • Display and measure advertising where enabled.
  • Comply with legal obligations and respond to support requests.

3. Service providers

Wattly uses Google Sign-In, Firebase services (including Authentication and Cloud Messaging), and Google AdMob. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. We do not sell your personal information.

4. Sharing and retention

We share information with providers that help us operate Wattly, when required by law, or to protect users and the service. We retain information only as long as reasonably needed to provide Wattly, meet legal requirements, resolve disputes, and prevent fraud or abuse.

5. Your choices and account deletion

You may manage notification and advertising preferences through your device and Google settings. You may permanently delete your account using our account deletion page. Requests are authenticated and sent to our backend. Information may be retained where required by law or necessary for security.

6. Children

Wattly is not directed to children under 13, or the minimum age required in your country. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Security

We use reasonable safeguards, including authenticated access and encrypted connections. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Changes

We may update this policy when our services or legal obligations change. We will publish the updated version here and update the effective date.
